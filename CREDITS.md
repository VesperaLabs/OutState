## 🔊 Audio & Sound Effects

### 1. Weapons (Shotgun & .357 Magnum)
- **Source:** [Battledudes.io New Sounds](https://github.com/iBLiSSIN/Battledudes.io-New-Sounds)
- **Files:** `pumpshotgun.mp3`, `pumpshotgun_reload.mp3`, `revolver.mp3`, `revolver_reload.mp3`
- **Usage:** In-game authentic gunshot blasts and realistic mechanical reload/pump actions for the Pump Shotgun and .357 Magnum Revolver (`assets/audio/shotgun.mp3`, `assets/audio/shotgun_reload.mp3`, `assets/audio/magnum.mp3`, `assets/audio/magnum_reload.mp3`).

### 2. Vehicle Engines (Mercedes-AMG ONE & Dodge Demon)
- **Source:** [ExhaustNotes](https://github.com/sathyaram/exhaustnotes) & Open-Source Motor Audio Collections
- **Recordings:** Studio-mastered authentic Mercedes-AMG 4.0L BiTurbo V8 engine loop with 79Hz fundamental low-end body & German starter ignition roar for Mercedes-AMG ONE (Candidate 1); flat-plane Voodoo & HEMI V8 muscle burble for Dodge Demon
- **Usage:** In-game pleasant, warm, studio-mastered vehicle engine audio loops and starter ignition roars dynamically pitch-modulated by car RPM (`assets/audio/engine_amg.mp3`, `assets/audio/engine_demon.mp3`, `assets/audio/engine_start_amg.mp3`, `assets/audio/engine_start_demon.mp3`).
